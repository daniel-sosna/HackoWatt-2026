Plain-language glossary

Every technical term used by the simulator.

PV · photovoltaicsSolar panels that convert sunlight directly into electricity.
kWp · kilowatt-peakThe rated DC power of the panels under standard laboratory conditions. It is power, not annual energy.
kWh · kilowatt-hourA unit of energy. A 1 kW appliance running for one hour uses 1 kWh.
Self-consumptionThe share of PV production used immediately inside the home instead of exported.
Demand coverageThe share of household electricity demand supplied directly by PV at the same time.
Grid import / exportElectricity bought from the grid / surplus electricity sent to the grid.
CAPEXCapital expenditure: the up-front purchase and installation cost.
OPEXOperating expenditure: recurring maintenance and operating cost.
Payback periodInitial net cost divided by annual savings. It does not by itself measure lifetime value.
NPV · net present valueAll future savings and costs discounted into today's money, less the initial investment.
COP · coefficient of performanceHeat delivered divided by electricity used. Direct electric heating is about 1; a heat pump can be above 1 and changes with conditions. This house currently uses direct electric heating, so no heat-pump COP is applied.
Net-billingA value-based settlement for exported energy. In Poland the credit depends on market settlement rules rather than the fixed Hackathon export rate.
Source data: . Results are scenarios based on a synthetic, uncalibrated household and stated assumptions, not a financial offer or performance guarantee.
